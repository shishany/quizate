# Quiz & Rewards Platform — Saudi Regulatory Dossier

Version 1.0 · 30 Oct 2026 · AlgoNova Technologies

The owner's monthly cash leaderboard does not fit the published Saudi commercial-competition rules as they stand. Those rules allow a limited number of licensed competitions a year, each lasting no more than 60 days and entered once. The free trivia game with sponsors can launch. Cash prizes need either a written ruling that a skill league falls outside those rules, or a redesign into licensed seasonal campaigns. This is a planning dossier for counsel, not a legal opinion.

## 1. What the published rules say

Five of the published conditions directly contradict the owner's model: two licences a year, a 60-day limit, one entry per participant, a ban on purchase-to-enter, and draws under Ministry supervision. Rows marked _reported_ come from press coverage of the Ministerial rules and must be checked against the official text by counsel.

| Published condition                                                                                      | Source                                                                                  | What it means for this concept                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Electronic licence from the Chamber of Commerce before the competition; licence number shown in all ads  | [MoC, 21 Sep 2025](https://mc.gov.sa/en/mediacenter/News/Pages/21-09-25-01.aspx)        | Organizer must hold a CR; each prize competition needs its own permit      |
| Entry method, prizes and start/end dates must be stated                                                  | MoC, 21 Sep 2025                                                                        | Official rules must be published in the app before launch                  |
| Prize draw held under Ministry of Commerce supervision                                                   | MoC, 21 Sep 2025                                                                        | Unclear how a score-ranked league (no draw) is supervised — question Q-03  |
| No purchase may be required to enter                                                                     | MoC, 21 Sep 2025                                                                        | The SAR 7 annual subscription for entry would breach this                  |
| Organizer, relatives and employees may not take part                                                     | MoC, 21 Sep 2025                                                                        | Staff, sponsor staff and families excluded (BRD BR-15)                     |
| Prizes delivered within 7 days of the draw; results announced on the same channels                       | MoC, 21 Sep 2025                                                                        | Winner KYC must be complete before month close                             |
| No more than 60 days from announcement to prize delivery                                                 | MoC, 21 Sep 2025                                                                        | A permanent, always-on leaderboard cannot be one competition               |
| Each establishment may hold a competition licence twice a year                                           | Ministerial rules, _reported_ by [Al Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1123258) | 12 monthly prize rounds a year exceeds this by 10                          |
| A contestant may not enter a competition more than once                                                  | Same, _reported_                                                                        | Conflicts directly with many matches per month                             |
| Questions must not breach Sharia principles; travel prizes must be inside the Kingdom                    | Same, _reported_                                                                        | Content review checklist; prize catalogue rule                             |
| Chamber fee per application (SAR 250 reported; SAR 500 listed by one Chamber)                            | Same, _reported_; study v1.0                                                            | Immaterial cost; verify the current amount                                 |
| Unlicensed competitions are prosecuted under the Anti-Commercial Fraud Law; violators are publicly named | [Arab News, Sep 2024](https://arab.news/v4tay)                                          | Launching prizes without a permit is a criminal-law risk for the organizer |
| Gambling and lotteries are prohibited                                                                    | General Saudi law                                                                       | Any element of paid chance (paid entry + luck) must be excluded            |

These ratings are AlgoNova's project risk ratings, not legal classifications.

| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |

| Sponsored (branded) questions, clearly labelled                    | Advertising                                                                  | Low–medium  | Disclosure label; sponsor content review              |

| Prizes funded by sponsors, run by the sponsor under its own permit | Commercial competition by the sponsor                                        | Medium      | Sponsor holds permit; platform is technology provider |

| Points convertible to cash or transferable value                   | Possible stored value / payment activity (SAMA)                              | High        | Excluded; points have no cash value                   |
| Prizes to players outside Saudi Arabia                             | Foreign law in each country, plus payment and sanctions screening            | High        | Saudi residents only at launch                        |
| Vouchers or sponsor products instead of cash                       | Still a prize competition; lower value, same permit rules                    | Medium      | Can replace cash inside licensed campaigns            |

## 3. Routes to market

Recommended: launch Route A, sell Route C to sponsors from the first quarter, and pursue Route B in parallel. Only Route B could deliver the owner's monthly prize, and it may be refused.

| Route                           | What it is                                                                                                                                                                           | Legal fit                          | Commercial effect                                                                     | Model setting                              |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| A — Engagement only             | Free game, leaderboards, badges, sponsored questions, ads; no cash prizes                                                                                                            | Strong                             | No prize cost; base case improves by about SAR 1.1M over 36 months                    | Prize switch = 0                           |
| B — Skill-league ruling         | Obtain a written position that a free, skill-ranked league with fixed prizes is outside the commercial-competition regime, or licensed under another (e.g. sports/esports) framework | Unknown                            | Owner's concept intact                                                                | Prize switch = 1, start month after ruling |
| C — Sponsor-licensed campaigns  | Each sponsor runs its own licensed campaign (up to its own annual allowance, ≤ 60 days) on the platform; sponsor funds the prizes                                                    | Good, if each campaign is licensed | Prize cost shifts to sponsors; becomes a premium product (SAR 15–40K plus prize pool) | Sponsor-funded share up to 100%            |
| D — Platform seasonal campaigns | The owner's entity runs about two licensed seasons a year (e.g. Ramadan, National Day), ≤ 60 days each, with Ministry-supervised selection                                           | Good                               | Two prize rounds of SAR 55K = SAR 110K a year instead of SAR 660K                     | Prize months limited                       |

Until Route B is answered, the owner should present the monthly SAR 55,000 to players as "planned", never as promised.

## 6. Official rules outline, roadmap and sources

The official competition rules, published in the app before any prize round, should contain:

1. Organizer's legal name, CR number and permit number.
2. Competition dates (announcement to prize delivery ≤ 60 days, unless Q-01 says otherwise).
3. Free entry statement; no purchase required or increases chances.
4. Eligibility: age 18+, Saudi residency, exclusions (organizer, relatives, staff, sponsor staff).
5. How to take part, and how scores and ranks are calculated (BR-01 to BR-10).
6. Prize table, prize form (bank transfer), and the 7-day delivery commitment.
7. Verification, disqualification, fair-play and appeal procedures.
8. How and where results are announced.
9. Personal data use for the competition and winner publication.
10. Statement that Apple and Google are not sponsors and are not involved.
11. Complaints contact and governing law.

Approval roadmap:

1. Weeks 1–2: owner names the organizing entity; counsel engaged; Q-01 to Q-11 filed.
2. Weeks 2–6: written answers collected; route chosen (B, C or D); Gate G1 decision.
3. During build: PDPL documentation, GAMR rating application, store consultations.
4. Before launch: Route A goes live with prizes switched off (Gate G5).
5. After G1: first licensed prize round, with penetration test and payout dry run.
